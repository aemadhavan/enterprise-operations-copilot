# Data Retention Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets minimum and maximum retention periods so that Kestrel keeps records long enough to meet legal obligations, and no longer.

## 2. Retention schedule
| Record type | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Customer PII | 7 years after the customer relationship ends, then deleted |
| Access request records (including approvals) | 7 years |
| Access logs for Restricted systems | 2 years |
| Service desk tickets | 3 years |
| Employee and contractor records | 7 years after the engagement ends |

## 3. Deletion
Records past their retention period are deleted or anonymised within 90 days. The data owner confirms deletion to the Privacy Office.

## 4. Legal hold
The Privacy Officer may suspend deletion for records under legal hold.
