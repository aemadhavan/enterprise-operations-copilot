# Contractor Onboarding and Offboarding Procedure

**Onboarding process owner:** Grace Whitfield (IT Service Management)
**Offboarding process owner:** Arjun Mehta (Identity & Access)
**Governing policies:** POL-022, VND-002

## Onboarding
1. The sponsor raises an onboarding ticket in Harbor Service Desk at least **5 business days** before the contractor's start date.
2. If the contractor works for a vendor, the sponsor confirms that the vendor's Supplier Security Assessment (VND-002) is valid for the whole engagement.
3. People Systems creates the contractor record in Pulse HRIS, including the contract end date.
4. The contractor signs the confidentiality agreement (CTL-03) and completes security awareness training (CTL-04).
5. For roles with access to Restricted data, People Systems completes a background check (CTL-09).
6. Identity & Access creates the account in Gatekeeper IAM with an end date, and enrols the contractor in MFA (CTL-01).
7. Access to specific systems is requested separately through PRC-012.
8. On day 1, the sponsor confirms that induction is complete.

## Offboarding
9. The sponsor raises an offboarding ticket in Harbor Service Desk before the contract end date.
10. Identity & Access disables all accounts in Gatekeeper IAM within 24 hours of the end date (POL-022 §6).
11. People Systems closes the contractor record in Pulse HRIS.
