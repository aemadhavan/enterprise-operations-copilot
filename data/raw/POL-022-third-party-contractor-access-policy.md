# Third-Party and Contractor Access Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to contractors, consultants and vendor staff who need access to Kestrel systems or data.

## 2. Prerequisites before any access is granted
- **2.1** A signed confidentiality agreement (CTL-03).
- **2.2** Security awareness training completed within the last 12 months (CTL-04).
- **2.3** A background check, where the access includes Restricted data (CTL-09).
- **2.4** A nominated Kestrel sponsor (the line manager), who is accountable for the contractor's access.

## 3. Duration
Contractor accounts must have an end date that matches the contract end date. Access to Restricted data is limited to a maximum of 90 days per approval (CTL-02).

## 4. Privileged access
Contractors must not be granted privileged (administrator) access to production systems that hold Restricted data. Any exception must follow the Privileged Access Management Policy (POL-041) and be approved by the Head of Security Governance.

## 5. Vendor organisations
A vendor's staff may receive access only while the vendor holds a valid Supplier Security Assessment (VND-002).

## 6. Offboarding
All contractor access must be revoked within **24 hours** of the engagement ending. The sponsor must raise an offboarding ticket before the end date.
