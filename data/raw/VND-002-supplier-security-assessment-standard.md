# Supplier Security Assessment Standard

## 1. When an assessment is required
Any vendor whose staff will access Confidential or Restricted data must hold a valid Supplier Security Assessment.

## 2. Assessment content
- A completed security questionnaire.
- Evidence of **ISO/IEC 27001 certification** or a **SOC 2 Type II report**, issued within the last 12 months.

## 3. Rating and validity
- Vendors are rated Low, Medium or High risk.
- High-risk vendors require Security Governance sign-off and an agreed remediation plan.
- An assessment is valid for **12 months**. When it expires, the vendor's staff cannot be granted new access until it is renewed.
