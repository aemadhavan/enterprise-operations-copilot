# Access Request and Approval Procedure

**Process owner:** Arjun Mehta (Identity & Access)
**Governing policies:** POL-014, POL-022, POL-041

## 1. Submitting a request
Requests are submitted through the "Access Request" form in Harbor Service Desk. Each request must include: requester name and type (employee or contractor), sponsor, target system, data category, access level (read, write or admin), business justification and duration.

## 2. Determining approvals
Gatekeeper IAM determines the required approvals from the Approval Matrix (REF-005), based on the requester type and the highest classification of the target system (REF-003).

## 3. Approval sequence
Approvals are collected in this order: line manager → data owner → Security Governance → Privacy Officer (where required).

## 4. Service levels
- Standard requests: approved or rejected within **2 business days**.
- Requests involving Restricted data: within **5 business days**.

## 5. Provisioning
Identity & Access provisions access only after every required approval is recorded.

## 6. Records
Access request records, including approvals, are retained according to the Data Retention Policy (POL-030).

## 7. Emergency access
In a genuine operational emergency, Security Governance may grant temporary access for up to **24 hours**. Emergency access is reviewed on the next business day, and a full request must then be submitted.
